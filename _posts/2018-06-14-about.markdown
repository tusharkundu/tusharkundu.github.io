**Welcome!**

**I am an economist and data scientist based in Alameda, California.**

I am Research Lead at [Planet Reimagined](https://www.planetreimagined.com/) and a Data Scientist at [Learning Collider](https://www.learningcollider.org/). I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Columbia University (2026).

As an applied microeconomist, I study questions in **development, labor, education, and algorithmic decision-making**, using causal inference, structural modeling, experiments, and mixed methods.

Before Columbia, I completed my B.A. in Economics and Math at Swarthmore College (2017) and worked as a research assistant at USC and the NBER.  

Outside of research, I enjoy biking, climbing, and cooking the occasional biryani. My simple pleasures include watching stand up comedy, instant coffee (sue me), sunny rooms, and saffron.
